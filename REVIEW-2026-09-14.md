# UI/UX & Feature Review: Loose Change
**Date:** 2026-09-14
**Stack:** Next.js 16 (PWA) + Convex + Clerk + AssemblyAI, TypeScript
**Scope:** UI/UX and features, read through the ND-design lens. Other dimensions flagged in one line each.
**Previous reviews:** REVIEW-2026-07-22.md and REVIEW-2026-09-09.md. Every finding from both has shipped (all closed in ChaosPatch), so nothing below repeats them.
**Coverage:** every screen and shared component read in full (`src/components/*`, `src/app/*`, `public/sw.js`, manifest, `convex/push*.ts`, `convex/crons.ts`).

## Executive Summary

Capture is now genuinely strong: offline cold start, incremental chunk saves, a recording timer, drafts that survive, and stuck captures you can see. Triage respects executive function (Skip, swipe with text labels, no countdown).

The gaps have moved **downstream of capture**:
- A decision, once made, is hard to take back (only Discard has undo, and nothing can go back to the Inbox).
- Several actions fail without telling you.
- The words for the same thing change from screen to screen.
- The app is still mostly **eyes-on**, even though its headline use case is driving.

There's also a real opportunity in **more ways to capture**: sharing from other apps, and home-screen shortcuts.

Nothing here needs an architecture change. The biggest item is about half a day.

Two findings are inferred from code and not yet seen on a device. They're marked **(unverified)**.

## High Priority

### 1. Keep and Send can't be undone, and nothing can return to the Inbox
- **What**: In Triage only Discard gets an Undo toast (`TriageScreen.tsx:84-96`). Keep (`:79-82`) and every Send button commit silently, and the entry is gone from the queue. There is also no mutation that moves an entry back to `untriaged` except `undoDiscard`, so a kept or sent memo can never return to the Inbox from anywhere: not Triage, Archive or Search.
- **Why it matters**: The ND lens's rule is "undo everything". Fear of an irreversible mis-tap makes each decision cost more, and Triage is a stream of fast decisions, often swiped. A thumb slip on Keep currently means going to Archive, finding the memo, and still being unable to put it back.
- **Suggested action**:
  - Add a single Undo toast after *any* Triage action (Keep, Discard, Send), backed by a new `returnToInbox` mutation that sets `untriaged` and clears `triagedAt`/`promotedTo`.
  - Add "Move to Inbox" to Archive and Search cards.
  - Parity: the MCP ↔ UI parity test will require either an `lc_return_to_inbox` tool or an entry in the "deliberately app-only" list.
- **Effort**: Half-day

### 2. Recording isn't built for eyes-off use
- **What**: Nothing in `src` requests a screen wake lock or vibrates. The only confirmation that recording started or stopped is visual: the button turns red and pulses (the pulse is off with reduced motion), plus a small timer.
- **Why it matters**: The README's first scenario is capturing while driving. You can't look at the screen to check it's listening, and a phone that locks mid-memo in a mounted cradle may pause or end the recording. Mobile browsers generally suspend background audio capture **(unverified on your devices)**.
- **Suggested action**:
  - Request `navigator.wakeLock` while recording and release it on stop or cancel.
  - Vibrate briefly on start and double on stop, where supported (Android; iOS Safari doesn't support it).
  - Consider an opt-in start/stop tone in Settings, off by default per the sensory principle.
- **Effort**: Half-day

### 3. Actions fail without telling you, and offline screens just say "Loading…"
- **What**: These all call mutations with no error handling:
  - Triage: `handleKeep`, `handleDiscard`, `handleUndo`, and `markPromoted` after a successful copy
  - Archive: Restore and Delete (`KeptScreen.tsx:55-65,123`)
  - Search: Keep, Discard and Restore (`SearchScreen.tsx:93-109`), all fired with `void`

  A rejection leaves a button that looks dead. Separately, no screen checks whether you're online, so Inbox, Archive, Search and Triage show "Loading…" with no explanation when there's no connection **(unverified: expected from Convex's reconnecting websocket, not reproduced)**.
- **Why it matters**: "No silent failures, no mystery state" is a core predictability rule, and the app already applies it well to clipboard and sync. A Keep that didn't register leaves an entry that reappears later with no explanation.
- **Suggested action**:
  - One small shared helper that runs a mutation and shows a toast naming the action on failure ("Couldn't keep that — try again").
  - An app-wide offline strip on the secondary screens ("You're offline — your inbox and archive will load when you reconnect. Recording still works.") driven by `online`/`offline` events.
- **Effort**: Half-day

## Medium Priority

### 4. Signed-out captures sync into whoever signs in next
- **What**: The offline queue (`offlineQueue.ts`) isn't scoped to a user, and Sign out (`SettingsScreen.tsx:120`) neither checks nor clears it. Captures still waiting when you sign out sync under the next account that signs in on that device.
- **Why it matters**: You have two accounts. A memo captured on one could land in the other, and there's no warning before signing out with unsynced captures.
- **Suggested action**:
  - Store `userId` on each queued capture and only sync your own.
  - Before signing out with captures still pending, show "N captures haven't synced yet. Signing out keeps them on this device until you sign back in."
- **Effort**: Half-day

### 5. The same thing has three names, and "Sent on" doesn't say where
- **What**:
  - `promotedTo` is never shown anywhere in the UI (grep: zero hits in `src` outside the MCP layer), so "Sent on" entries don't say which app they went to.
  - `EntryCard` prints the raw status (`· promoted`, `· untriaged`) in Search and on the reminder card, while the tabs call these "Sent on" and "Inbox".
  - Archive's trash button is labelled "Delete" and its toast says "Deleted", but it's the same 30-day discard that Triage and Search call "Discard".
- **Why it matters**: The rules are consistent terminology and precise language. "Deleted" also suggests it's permanent when it isn't, which is the reverse of the reassurance undo should give. And "where did I send that?" is exactly the working-memory gap this app is meant to cover.
- **Suggested action**:
  - One status → label map used everywhere (Inbox / Kept / Sent on / Discarded).
  - Show "Sent to ControlledChaos" on sent entries.
  - Rename Archive's action and toast to Discard / Discarded.
- **Effort**: Quick fix

### 6. Undo and feedback are uneven across screens
- **What**:
  - Discarding from Search has no Undo toast (Triage and Archive both have one).
  - Restore (Archive, Search) gives no confirmation, even though `undoDiscard` returns where the entry went.
  - The Discarded tab doesn't show how long until an entry is gone for good, and the 6-second Triage toast doesn't mention the 30-day Restore in Archive.
- **Why it matters**: Same button, same behaviour, everywhere (predictability). Showing the remaining time before permanent deletion is time-awareness scaffolding, and it turns a hidden deadline into visible information.
- **Suggested action**:
  - Shared undo toast on every Discard.
  - "Restored to Kept" confirmation.
  - "Gone for good in 12 days" on Discarded cards.
  - Toast copy: "Discarded · Undo (or restore from Archive for 30 days)".
- **Effort**: Quick fix

### 7. Navigation runs through a single row of small icons on Inbox
- **What**: Search, Triage, Archive and Settings are reachable only from the Inbox header, as 18px icons with 10px labels (`InboxScreen.tsx:51-65`). Every other screen's only link is back to Inbox, so Archive → Search is two hops, and so is Record → Archive. Triage, the Inbox's obvious next action, looks exactly like Settings. The header is likely cramped or overflowing at phone width **(unverified)**.
- **Why it matters**:
  - Consistent navigation in the same place on every screen is a core predictability rule.
  - The right next action should be obvious, and "Triage 12" deserves to be the Inbox's primary button, not one of four equal icons.
- **Suggested action**:
  - A small persistent nav (Inbox / Triage / Archive / Search, with Settings in the corner) on every screen **except Record**, which keeps its one primary action.
  - A full-width "Triage N" button at the top of a non-empty Inbox.
- **Effort**: Half-day
- **Needs a decision** (see Questions).

### 8. Transcripts can't be corrected
- **What**: No screen can edit a transcript. A garbled voice transcript or a typo in a text capture stays that way permanently. Search, the reminder, and the text copied to other apps all use it.
- **Why it matters**: Search is the app's retrieval tool, and a misheard key word makes a memo unfindable. Sending a garbled transcript to ControlledChaos means fixing it there instead, every time.
- **Suggested action**: An Edit action on an expanded card (Inbox, Triage, Archive), saved inline. Keep the original if you want a way to revert. Parity: `lc_update_transcript`, or add it to the app-only list.
- **Effort**: Half-day
- **Goes beyond the current spec.**

### 9. More ways in: share to Loose Change, and home-screen shortcuts
- **What**: The manifest has no `share_target` and no `shortcuts`. To capture something you're looking at in another app (a link, a highlighted sentence), you have to open Loose Change, switch to text mode, and paste. There's also no way to jump straight into text mode.
- **Why it matters**: The app's premise is starting with as little friction as possible. Sharing from another app and "long-press the icon → Type a thought" each remove two or three steps at the exact moment a thought is about to slip away.
- **Suggested action**:
  - `share_target` (GET, `title`/`text`/`url` params) → a small route that queues a text capture with the same `localId` flow and shows "Saved ✓".
  - Manifest `shortcuts` for "Record" and "Type a thought" (`/?mode=text`).
  - Note that iOS supports neither, so this mainly helps Android and desktop.
- **Effort**: Half-day
- **Goes beyond the current spec.**

### 10. Reminders are one fixed time for everyone
- **What**: `crons.ts` sends every reminder on Sunday at 16:00 UTC; the comment says this was deliberate at one user. Settings only has on/off, and a denied permission says "Notification permission denied." with no next step.
- **Why it matters**: The ND notification rule is user control over frequency and timing, including quiet hours. A resurfacing nudge that lands at a bad moment gets dismissed unread, which defeats its purpose.
- **Suggested action**:
  - Store day, time and timezone per subscription, and run an hourly cron that sends to subscriptions whose local time matches.
  - Offer weekly / every few days / off.
  - Permission copy: explain how to re-enable notifications in browser or OS settings.
- **Effort**: Half-day
- **Needs a decision** (see Questions).

### 11. No way to get your captures out
- **What**: No export exists in the UI (MCP clients can read entries one at a time).
- **Why it matters**: These are personal, accumulating thoughts. A one-tap backup is both a trust feature and an escape hatch, and the ND skill lists export as a baseline quality-of-life feature.
- **Suggested action**: Settings → "Download all captures" as Markdown (one section per entry, with status, date, destination) or JSON. Transcripts only; audio links expire anyway.
- **Effort**: Half-day

## Low Priority

### 12. Text and tap targets are small for a one-handed app
- Transcripts in cards are `text-sm` (14px), metadata `text-xs`, and the Inbox nav labels `text-[10px]`. The ND typography baseline is 16px for body text.
- The Archive and Search trash buttons are `p-2` around a 16px icon (~32px). Restore and Keep are text-xs underlined links, about 16px tall.
- Triage's "→ CC" is an abbreviation; the full name already exists in `DESTINATIONS`.
- **Quick fix.**

### 13. Triage doesn't show how many are left
- The Inbox shows a count, but Triage shows nothing. A plain "7 left" (no progress bar, no framing) makes the batch feel finite, which is the chunking principle.
- **Quick fix.**

### 14. Search and Archive forget where you were
- Search is silently capped at 20 results with no "showing first 20" note (`entries.ts` `limit ?? 20`).
- The search query, the Archive tab and the page number reset whenever you navigate away. Put them in the URL so Back returns you to the same place.
- There's no visible search button; submitting relies on Enter or the keyboard's Go.
- **Quick fix.**

### 15. Smaller items
- **The mic error isn't actionable.** "Microphone access needed to record." should say how to allow it.
- **Text mode has no keyboard save.** Add ⌘/Ctrl+Enter.
- **Retention is only explained after it happens.** "Audio was cleared 30 days after triage" appears only once the audio is gone. A short "What happens to your captures" line in Settings (audio kept 30 days after triage; discarded memos removed after 30 days) avoids the surprise.
- **Sending needs a manual app switch.** After "Copied — paste into ControlledChaos", an "Open ControlledChaos" link in the toast would save that step (see Questions).
- **Two toasts collide in Triage.** The discard toast and the copied toast share `bottom-24`, so discarding then sending stacks them on top of each other.

## Strengths
- **Both previous reviews fully shipped**, including the ND pass: labelled nav, reduced motion, a recording timer, and swipe intent shown in text as well as colour.
- **Capture resilience is excellent**: offline cold start, incremental chunk saves, orphan salvage, visible stuck captures, backoff.
- **No shame mechanics, anywhere.** Counts are plain, the reminder resurfaces a kept idea instead of nagging about the backlog, and Skip exists.
- **Comments record decisions and their reasons**, which is why this review could go straight to UX instead of reconstructing intent.

## Decisions (Nae, 2026-09-14)
1. **Navigation (#7):** persistent nav bar on every screen except Record, plus a prominent "Triage N" button on the Inbox.
2. **Editing transcripts (#8):** yes, and keep the original so an edit can be reverted.
3. **Reminders (#10):** add day/time/frequency controls, and fix the permission-denied copy.
4. **Sending (#15):** unknown whether Kindling or ControlledChaos accept prefilled text, so keep the clipboard handoff and add an "Open [app]" link to the toast.
5. **Unverified items:** still open. Check the Inbox header on a phone, and whether Archive hangs on "Loading…" offline. Both are logged in Tangle.
